Trust

What LDTT is — and what it is not.

Is

Portable trust stamp for drone software connectors (tools/plugins that talk to a drone).

Observe = first trust level: declared scope, read-oriented limits, revocable. About the connector, not flight clearance.

Is not

  • Aircraft certification
  • Operator certification / licensing
  • Airworthiness
  • Flight safety / “safe to fly”
  • FAA or other regulatory claim
  • Fleet dashboard

Checked · Declared · Revocable

What was checked (Observe)

At Observe, the intent is: no malicious/write-heavy scope for that level, declared limits (what it may send/receive), and build/provenance details in order for that stamp record — so you have a clearer basis to run the connector. Exact controls live in the stamp spec.

Revocable: Yes. LDTT is designed so a bad or outdated connector can be cut off with a signed revocation list.

Draft vs real issuance

Treat current reference artifacts as draft / placeholder until real Issuer + trust-root signing is live. Prefer “checked against the Observe reference” over “certified.”

Do not claim

  1. FAA approval, certification, or endorsement
  2. Any other civil aviation authority approval
  3. Aircraft certification or type design approval
  4. Operator certification or licensing
  5. Airworthiness
  6. Flight safety certification or “safe to fly”
  7. That LDTT replaces operational risk assessment, SOPs, or insurance requirements
  8. That a stamped connector makes the vehicle or mission compliant
  9. Military / weapons certification (out of scope entirely)
  10. Guarantees about third-party software beyond the stamp’s stated connector controls

Stamp + logo use

On-page: stamp with connector-scope context; logo in header/footer.

Off-page (intended): display stamp alongside a connector product/docs; link to verify / this page / Spec. Do not imply LDTT certifies the airframe or operator; do not use in FAA filings.
